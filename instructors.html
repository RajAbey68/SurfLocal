Meet the Family

We are a team of local brothers and cousins who grew up surfing these waves. We don't just teach you to stand up; we teach you to love the ocean.

Head Instructor / Founder

Sameera "Sammy" Perera

"I started surfing on a wooden plank when I was 5. The ocean is my home."

Sammy is ISA Level 2 certified and has over 15 years of teaching experience. He is known for his endless patience and knowing exactly when the big set is coming.

Favorite Spot: Kabalana Rock

Beginner Specialist

Lahiru

"The best surfer is the one having the most fun."

Lahiru is the king of high-fives. He specializes in making nervous beginners feel safe and confident. His smile is contagious!

Favorite Spot: Weligama Beach Break

Intermediate Guide

Kusun

"Style comes from relaxation."

If you want to learn how to turn and trim, Kusun is your guy. He is an amazing stylish longboarder who knows all the secret spots in Midigama.

Favorite Spot: Lazy Left
