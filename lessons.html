Common Questions

What should I bring?

Just your swimwear, sunscreen (zinc recommended!), and a towel. We provide the surfboards and rash guards.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

You should be comfortable in the water, but for beginner lessons, we stay in waist-deep water with sandy bottoms (Weligama Beach). You are safe with us!

What if the waves are too big?

South Sri Lanka is famous for beginner-friendly waves. If it's too big at one spot, we drive to a sheltered cove like "Lazy Left" or "Fisherman's" nearby.
