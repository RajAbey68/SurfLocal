South Coast Surf Guide

The Southern Province of Sri Lanka is a wave garden. From mellow beach breaks to hollow reefs, here are our favorite spots.

Weligama Beach Break

Level: Beginner Bottom: Sand Best Tide: All Tides

Weligama is the capital of beginner surfing in Sri Lanka. It's a massive, sheltered bay with a soft sandy bottom. The waves are gentle, rolling, and offer long rides perfect for learning to pop up.

Our Tip: It can get crowded. We take our students to a secret sandbank on the far left of the bay to avoid the masses.

Kabalana (The Rock)

Level: Intermediate / Advanced Bottom: Reef/Sand Best Tide: Mid to High

Known as "The Rock", this A-frame peak provides powerful waves for experienced surfers. However, the inside section (known as "Baby Rock") is fantastic for intermediates looking to transition from white water to green waves.

Midigama Lazy Left

Level: Intermediate Bottom: Deep Reef Best Tide: Low Tide

A classic longboarding wave. It breaks slowly over a deep reef, making it very safe for a reef break. Perfect for refining your turns and enjoying a long, peeling left-hander.
